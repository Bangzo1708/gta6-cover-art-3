# Vice Cover Studio — GTA 6 Cover Art

Turn your photos into a GTA 6-style cover art, right in the browser. Vice Cover Studio is an offline, browser-based tool that transforms a photo and a caption into a Vice City cover: multi-panel composition, neon-sunset grade, and PNG export in 3 sizes.

## What it does

- Upload a photo and type a caption — get a cover in the style of GTA 6 key art
- Multi-panel layout with the signature neon-sunset Vice City color grade
- Export your cover as PNG in 3 sizes (square, portrait, landscape)

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve the folder with any static server)
2. Upload a photo
3. Type your caption
4. Pick a layout and export as PNG in your preferred size

No install, no upload to a server — everything runs locally in your browser.
